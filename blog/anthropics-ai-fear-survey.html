Dominant Public Anxiety Around AI's Impact

Anthropic's 'Public Record' survey, conducted between November and December 2025 by YouGov, polled nearly 52,000 Americans aged 16 and above across all 50 states, DC, and Puerto Rico. This first general public-focused survey by Anthropic revealed that over half of respondents expressed fear of losing both their jobs and their independent thinking to AI. While top hopes included curing diseases (48%) and helping people with disabilities (36%), the anxieties around job displacement and cognitive reliance were widespread.

The Disconnect for Indie Builders and Emerging Markets

The survey data highlights a significant gap between the tech world's optimism and public apprehension. For indie builders, especially those targeting diverse socio-economic landscapes such as India, these fears are not theoretical. User adoption hinges not just on utility but on assuaging existential anxieties, requiring solutions to be framed as empowering rather than replacing human capabilities and enhancing rather than diminishing cognition.

Designing for Trust and Human Augmentation

This widespread public concern presents an opportunity for AI developers to differentiate by building solutions that explicitly address job security and augment human intelligence. The societal imperative for AI development is to focus on transparent, ethical 'AI for good' that demonstrably benefits human agency and livelihoods. This human-centric approach is particularly crucial in markets like India, where economic anxieties are often more pronounced, demanding careful consideration of AI's societal integration.
