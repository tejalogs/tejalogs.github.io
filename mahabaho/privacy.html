MAHABAHO

Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 November 2026

Mahabaho is a home workout app for Android, package com.tejalogs.mahabaho, developed by Tejalogs. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Mahabaho.

In short: Mahabaho does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Data we collect

None. Mahabaho does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Mahabaho keeps the following only on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your answers to the first-run questions: your goal, how active you are, and any areas you asked it to go easy on. These can include health information you choose to give, such as a sore back, high blood pressure, pregnancy or recent surgery. The app uses them only to pick gentler exercises.
  • Your workout history: workouts finished, sets and reps, your weekly goal and your level.
  • Your settings: sound and vibration cues, and reminder days and time.

This data never leaves your device. It is not included in Android's cloud backup. Profile → Delete my data erases your answers and workout history. Uninstalling Mahabaho, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything, settings included.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only if you turn on workout reminders. The app asks first, and the reminders are created on your phone.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): only to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): for the optional buzz on each rep.

Health and safety

Mahabaho gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose anything. If something hurts, stop, and check with a doctor if the pain is sharp, in a joint, or doesn't ease.

Google Play and Android

Mahabaho is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information such as installs and crash reports under its own privacy policy. Mahabaho itself receives none of this information.

Children

Mahabaho is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
