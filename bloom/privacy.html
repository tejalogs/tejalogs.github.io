BLOOM

Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 October 2026

Bloom is a live-wallpaper app for Android, package app.bloom.wallpapers, developed by Tejalogs. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Bloom.

In short: Bloom does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

Data we collect

None. Bloom does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Bloom keeps the following only on your phone, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your settings (theme, frame rate, battery and motion preferences).
  • Presets you save, favourites and recently used wallpapers.
  • Small preview images of wallpapers, cached to make browsing faster.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling Bloom, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Permissions

  • Set wallpaper (SET_WALLPAPER, SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS): to apply the wallpaper you choose.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): only to resume automatic wallpaper changes after a restart, if you turn that on.

If you use “Save image”, Bloom adds a picture to your Photos. It never reads your existing photos or files.

Google Play and Android

Bloom is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information such as installs and crash reports under its own privacy policy. Bloom's fonts may be provided by Google Play services on your device. Bloom itself receives none of this information.

Children

Bloom is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
