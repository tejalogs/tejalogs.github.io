AVYA

Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 August 2026

Ava is a film camera app developed by Tejalogs. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

What Ava collects

Nothing. Ava has no internet permission at all — it is not technically capable of sending your photos, or anything else, anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers.

Where your photos are saved

Every photo and video you capture is saved in two places on your phone:

  • Inside Ava — the copy your diary shows, in the app's private storage. Deleting Ava deletes it.
  • In your device gallery — a copy is also published to Pictures/Ava (and Movies/Ava for clips) so your photos appear in Google Photos, Gallery, and any other app you have given access to your media.

That second copy is a normal part of your phone's photo library. It is visible to other apps that can read your media, and if you have a cloud photo backup switched on, that service will back it up like any other picture you take. Ava itself never uploads anything.

Permissions

Camera — to see through the lens and capture photos and video.

Microphone — used only while you are recording a video in CINE mode, to record that clip's audio. Ava does not listen at any other time and never records audio on its own.

Storage (Android 9 and below only) — needed on older versions of Android to save the gallery copy described above. Newer versions do not require it.

Third parties

Ava uses no third-party analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising networks, and no Firebase services that transmit user data. No information is shared with anyone.

Children

Ava is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest version.
